Privacy

Kampong, Hatch and Oleh: small apps made for one family and its friends. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who we are

These apps are built and run privately by one person for family and friends. They are not a company product and are not sold. Questions or requests: duclongschlong@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares only your name, email and profile picture with us.
  • What you add: for example seeds and circles in Kampong, trips and requests in Oleh, and logs and appointments in Hatch.
  • Your check-ins at the well (Kampong): one mood a day and, if you like, a short line about what you are building. People in your circles can see your mood and that line (a child is seen only by their family circle). It also counts toward your kampong's level, together with what you make, use on more than one day and share.
  • Notes you send us with the Tell button, and a screenshot only if you choose to include one.
  • Anonymous app usage: while we test with family and friends, which screens and features are opened, which version you run, and the type of any error (never its details), so we can fix crashes and see what helps. It is counted under a random number kept on your device, not your name or account, and never includes what you or your children write, say or photograph.
  • In Hatch, the same anonymous usage events also say which Learn topic you read and that a check-in was done or skipped, an appointment added or a note sent, and an error report names which part of the app's code failed (never the error text). Never what you type or say, your photos, or anything you record: moods, symptoms, weights, sleep, dates, your due date, appointment details or note text.

How it is used

  • To run the apps for you and the people you choose to share with (your circle).
  • To improve the apps. The app maker, and the AI coding tools that help build fixes, read the notes you send with the Tell button. In Hatch you are told before you send one.
  • The town crier (Kampong, when it is switched on) writes a short daily summary for grown-ups of what really happened in their circles: first names and the names of things people made or are building. An AI service only rewords those facts; it never sees what a child wrote or did, anything private, or the words inside what people made.
  • We never sell your information, never show ads and never share it with anyone outside your circle, except the services below that run the apps.

Services that run the apps

  • Supabase stores accounts and app data (servers in Sydney, Australia).
  • Google for "Continue with Google" sign-in. Firebase (Google) delivers app installs to testers and notifications.
  • GitHub hosts the web apps' files (no personal data). PostHog (servers in the EU) receives anonymous usage events and error reports for Kampong, Oleh and Hatch.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather on Today and in the Planner. It receives only the coordinates of the city you pick (Singapore unless you change it), never your location, name or anything you plan.
  • Those usage counts reach PostHog through a small relay we run on Cloudflare. It stores nothing and removes your IP address before passing them on, so PostHog never sees it.

Your choices

  • You can see and edit what you added in each app.
  • You can delete your account and data from the app (Me → Delete my account), or by emailing us. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Hatch is not medical advice and is not a medical record.
  • Children use Kampong only when a parent in the family adds them, and see only their family circle.

If this page changes in a way that matters, the apps will say so in "What's new".
